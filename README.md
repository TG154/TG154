## About Me

I'm a student from Lahore who spends a probably unreasonable amount of time messing with computers, Linux, hardware, and software.

I like old hardware, open-source software, comics, music, and projects where I can actually understand what's happening underneath the interface.

Most of the time I'm either building something, breaking something, reading something, or trying to figure out why something broke.
